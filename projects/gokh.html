GoKH — Cambodia Trip Planner

A single-page app for planning a Cambodia trip with friends: pick destinations, add your crew, set dates, transport, stay and budget, build a day-by-day itinerary — then share the whole plan as one link. No account, no server.

Built by RIN Nairith, software developer in Phnom Penh, Cambodia.

The problem

Group trips in Cambodia are usually planned in chat threads: dates in one message, the budget in another, the route somewhere else, and nobody sure what was agreed.

GoKH puts the whole plan in one place and makes sharing frictionless — friends open a link and keep planning, without signing up for anything.

What I built

Destinations

20 destinations with suggested stay, best season, entry fees, tips and curated activities — or type in any other place.

Crew

Add, rename and remove travellers, with exactly one trip leader.

Smart itinerary

Days are generated from the date range and keep their activities when dates change; one-tap suggestions and route templates.

Budget split

USD or KHR, per-traveller split and contribution tracking.

Live weather

An Open-Meteo forecast appears for trips within the next 14 days.

One-link sharing

The entire trip is encoded in a share link; drafts autosave in the browser so a refresh never loses work.

Stack & key decisions

The trip lives in the link

Instead of a database, the full plan is serialised into the share URL. Sharing is instant, there is nothing to host or secure server-side, and every link is a snapshot that still works later.

Treat shared links as untrusted input

Because anyone can craft a link, incoming trip data is validated and sanitised before it is rendered, and the site ships with strict security headers.

Content as data

Destinations, activities and travel advice are plain data files based on a research report, so they can be kept current without touching UI code.

CI runs type-check, tests and build

Every change goes through vue-tsc, the unit/component test suite and a production build before it can deploy.
